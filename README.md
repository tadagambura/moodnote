# MoodNote

> **Merged into [Reset](https://github.com/tadagambura/reset).**

Quick check-ins (mood, energy, social battery) now live inside Reset, and you can share them with your household or friend circles. Free apps like How We Feel cover private mood journals well.

Decided in the November 2026 portfolio review, based on market research across Reddit, forums,
TikTok/YouTube trends and app-store reviews. This repo is kept for history and is no longer developed.

# MoodNote

**A 30-second check-in that shows your patterns**

> #9 of 33 · Wave 1: Daily keepers · Used: Daily · Medium · Revenue potential: Medium · Habits, Health & Learning

## What it is

Log mood, energy, sleep and stress in one tap a day, add optional tags (work, exercise, period, medication), and see what lines up with your good and bad days. Export your history for a GP or therapist.

## Why it's in the portfolio

Daily use and genuinely useful insights. Positioned as practical pattern-tracking, not therapy, in a crowded but large market.

## Who it's for

People managing stress, burnout, ADHD, cycle symptoms or anxiety, or simply wanting more self-awareness.

**The problem:** It's hard to spot what affects your mood and energy, or to explain it to a professional.

## Version one

- One-tap daily check-in: mood, energy, sleep, stress
- Optional tags and a short note
- Weekly patterns
- Exportable history
- Gentle reminders, with no streak pressure

**Example:** A user notices that poor sleep and skipped lunches come before most of their low-energy days.

## Later

- Correlations ('low energy follows short sleep')
- Cycle-aware view
- Private journal lock

## Animation and tone

**Calm.** Smooth, reassuring motion only: gentle ticks, numbers easing into place. No characters, jokes or drama.

- Soft, slow transitions, like breathing

**Line we don't cross:** No celebrations or reactions to mood scores, ever.

## AI

**Light (opt-in).** Optional weekly reflection written from your check-ins. Opt-in only, premium, no identifying data sent.

## Watch-outs

Sensitive health data: no ad tracking on it, strong privacy, no medical claims, and signposting to real support. Deliberately no guilt streaks.
